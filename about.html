About us

We are a party planning and event decoration company based in Freeport, New York, dedicated to creating memorable celebrations for every special occasion. From intimate gatherings to large events, we focus on turning ideas into beautifully designed experiences.

We specialize in event décor and rentals, including tables, chairs, tents, balloon arrangements, and customized decorations for birthdays, baby showers, weddings, and more. Our goal is to make the planning process easy and stress-free, while delivering high-quality setups that reflect your style and vision.
